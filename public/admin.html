Gmail or Google Workspace

  1. Open your Google account security page and turn on 2-Step Verification.
  2. Open App passwords, type any name and press Create.
  3. Copy the 16 letters into App password here, then press Connect and send test.

If App passwords is missing on a company account, your Google Workspace administrator has to allow it.

Zoho Mail

  1. Open Zoho app passwords and generate one.
  2. Paste it here. Some free Zoho plans do not allow sending from other apps.

The account is saved only if the test email goes through. It is sent to the same address. Each account has a daily sending limit set by its provider.
